Privacy Policy

Bubbles App Manager is designed to work locally. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, telemetry, cloud sync, remote servers, or Internet permission.

SummaryLocal processingStorage reviewRetentionPermissionsYour controls

1. Summary

Bubbles processes application metadata, favorites, preferences, device-wide performance measurements, and user-authorized file information on your Android device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

2. Information processed locally

To provide its features, Bubbles may process:

  • Names, package identifiers, icons, versions, installation dates, and system/user classification for applications Android permits it to discover.
  • Device-wide RAM, storage, battery, charging, power-saver, uptime, and thermal measurements exposed by public Android APIs.
  • Favorites, theme choices, widget settings, monitoring choices, and other application preferences.
  • Bounded monitoring samples and optimization summaries when local history is enabled.

This information is not sent off the device.

3. Optional storage review

Storage review begins only after you select a folder with Android’s system folder picker. Inside that authorized folder, Bubbles may examine file names, sizes, modification dates, and locally computed SHA-256 hashes to identify large, old, or byte-identical duplicate candidates.

Bubbles never automatically deletes personal files. A file is deleted only after you select it and confirm the action. You can use “Forget folder access” to release the saved folder grant when the document provider permits it.

4. Memory Care and protected Android actions

Memory Care deletes only Bubbles-owned temporary cache files. Bubbles cannot inspect or clear another application’s memory or cache. App info, Force Stop, permission, notification, storage, battery, and uninstall interfaces opened by Bubbles are owned and controlled by Android.

5. Local retention and backup

Optional monitoring retains no more than 96 device-wide samples. You can disable monitoring or history and delete stored history from the Performance screen. Settings may be included in Android’s encrypted device backup or device transfer. Monitoring and optimization history are excluded from backup.

Uninstalling Bubbles or clearing its application data removes its local data, subject to Android’s backup and restoration behavior.

6. Permissions

Notifications
Optional warnings and the user-visible ongoing background-status notification on supported Android versions.
Foreground service
Supports the optional, visible “Keep running in background” status session after Bubbles leaves Recent Apps.
System-scheduled work
Android WorkManager may use wake-lock and restart support for user-enabled periodic local work.

Bubbles does not request Internet, broad all-packages inventory, usage access, all-files access, Accessibility Service, root, silent install/uninstall, or battery-optimization exemption permissions.

7. Your controls

  • Disable optional notifications, monitoring, history, or background mode in Settings.
  • Delete monitoring history from the Performance screen.
  • Forget an authorized storage folder from Storage Cleaner.
  • Clear Bubbles data or uninstall the app through Android settings.

8. Children, accounts, and international transfers

Bubbles does not provide accounts or intentionally collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no information is transmitted to the developer, Bubbles does not perform international data transfers.

9. Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. For support or privacy questions, use the public support options on the Support page.
