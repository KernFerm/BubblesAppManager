Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm the device runs Android 10 or later.
  2. Install the latest available Bubbles version.
  3. Restart Bubbles and repeat the action once.
  4. Check whether Android or the device manufacturer displayed a restriction.

When opening an issue, include the Android version, device manufacturer/model, Bubbles version, expected result, actual result, and safe reproduction steps. Do not post passwords, signing keys, personal files, full app inventories, private screenshots, or other sensitive information.

Common questions

Why does Force Stop open Android App info?

Android does not let an ordinary third-party app silently force-stop another app. Bubbles opens the exact selected application’s Android-owned App info screen so you can use the controls your device provides.

Why does uninstall require confirmation?

Every uninstall request is handled by Android’s confirmation interface. Bubbles never silently removes an application.

Why are some installed apps missing?

Android package visibility rules limit which apps Bubbles may discover. Bubbles intentionally avoids broad all-packages access and does not claim to show a complete inventory.

Why is system-wide Clean Now unavailable?

Android 14 and newer restrict process cleanup to the calling app. Older supported versions do not expose enough information to identify every protected active process or confirm a safe result. Bubbles therefore makes no cross-app attempt. The separate local action releases only Bubbles-owned temporary resources and reports real before-and-after device readings.

Why can background monitoring stop?

The optional foreground service is visible and stoppable, but Android, the user, or an OEM battery manager can still terminate it. Reopening Bubbles restarts it when the setting remains enabled.

Delete local data

Bubbles has no online account, so there is no server-side account or cloud profile to delete. To remove local information:

  • Delete monitoring history from the Performance screen.
  • Use “Forget folder access” in Storage Cleaner.
  • Disable history, monitoring, notifications, or background mode in Settings.
  • Use Android Settings → Apps → Bubbles App Manager → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Uninstall Bubbles to remove its local app data, subject to Android backup restoration behavior.

Security reports

Do not publish exploit details or secrets in a public issue. Open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel without including sensitive technical information.
